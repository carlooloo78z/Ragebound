    TACTICAL FIELD GUIDE

    01 / Keep your distance

    A jab is quick but short. Kicks reach farther. Step outside their reach, let an attack miss, then step in during RECOVERY for a 20% punish bonus.

    02 / Commit with purpose

    There is no stamina. Every move is available without a resource cost, but heavy attacks and specials still have recovery. Make opponents miss and punish them. You get one attack per jump; landing creates a short opening.

    03 / Read the guard

    Crouching strikes hit low: crouch + block. Aerial strikes and standing heavies hit overhead: stand + block. Neutral strikes can be blocked either way. Throws beat both guards but only work close up on the ground.

    04 / Turn defense into offense

    Block within 5 frames of contact for a perfect guard: no guard damage and a counterattack opening. Holding guard is safe against matching attacks until the guard meter breaks. Release it and retreat to recover.

    05 / Escape, then reset

    Press throw within 8 frames before a throw connects to escape it. Dash backward for brief evasion; dashing forward has no invulnerability. Dashes commit you to their full duration.

    06 / Learn your discipline

    Kung Fu recovers quickly. Boxing has fast footwork and an evasive heavy startup. Muay Thai controls kick range. Judo's heavy throws through guard. Jiu-Jitsu's heavy takedown adds longer ground control and guard pressure.

    Move names, timing labels and hitboxes are available only in Training. In matches, read the fighters’ animations. Choose best of 3, 5 or 7 above.
